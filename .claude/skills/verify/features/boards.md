# Boards

Crowsnest boards open as tabs in the Lead Studio workspace: Today (the morning screen), Funnel (driven in [funnel.md](funnel.md)), My channel (the own Instagram channel), Rivals (the tracked rivals), Plan (planned reels by month, week and day), Gate 1 (approve a proposed plan, driven in [baseline.md](baseline.md)) and Agents (the MCP journal and agent tokens), all read through the engine bridge. Each connector's state and key live in Settings > Sources, driven in [connectors.md](connectors.md). Architecture: `documents/architecture/boards.md`.

## Sub-features

- Board tab (`board-tab`), opened from the sidebar rows (`ls-nav-row`, `data-nav-id`) or the tab bar "+" menu, Lead Studio group (`new-tab-board`, `data-board-id`).
- App menu: top-level Boards menu, one item per board. On macOS the app menu itself is named Lead Studio, so boards get their own menu.
- Today: `today-offer`, `today-offer-step`, `today-funnel`, `today-step` (`data-step-id`), `today-drop` (`data-status`), `today-gates`, `today-open-gate`, `today-gate-plans`, `today-gate-drafts`, `today-pulls`, `today-pull-row` (`data-connector-id`, `data-state`, with a `state-pill`), `today-workspaces`, `today-workspace-row`; each block has one `source-tag`, and each number is a `kpi-value` (`data-state`: `value`, `partial`, `not-connected`, `pull-failed`, `pull-partial`, `no-data`) with a `kpi-state` badge, and `open-sources` on a not connected one.
- My channel: tabs `own-tab-home`, `own-tab-perf`, `own-tab-content`, `own-tab-aud` (panels `own-panel-*`), `own-days-7`, `own-days-30`, `own-stat-<metric>`, `own-total-<dm|gift_page_visit|purchase>`, `own-by-format`, `own-post-row` (`data-post-id`, `data-pull-error`) with `own-open-reel`, `own-post-pull-error` and `own-after-<key>` (`data-status`: `value`, `missing` or `no-keyword`), `own-sort-<key>`, `own-follower-split`, `own-audience-*`; reel view `own-reel-back`, `own-reel-open`, `own-reel-tab-ov`, `own-reel-tab-eng`, `own-reel-tab-after`, `own-reel-<metric>`, `own-reel-history`, `own-after-step` (`data-step-id`, `data-status`), `own-after-none`.
- Rivals: `rivals-channel-row` (`data-handle`), `rivals-outlier` (`data-video-id`), `rivals-outlier-open`, `rivals-evidence-card` (`data-video-id`), `rivals-evidence-open` (`data-url`), `rivals-add-input`, `rivals-add-submit`, `rivals-add-message` (`data-status`).
- Plan: views `plan-view-month`, `plan-view-week`, `plan-view-day`, `plan-prev`, `plan-today`, `plan-next`; Month `plan-month-day` (`data-date`, `data-in-month`), `plan-chip` (`data-plan-id`, `data-state`) with `plan-chip-tooltip` on hover, `plan-flag`, `plan-week` (`data-week-start`); Week `plan-week-day` (`data-date`), `plan-card` (`data-plan-id`, `data-state`, `data-movable`), `plan-time`; Day `plan-day-view` (`data-date`), `plan-detail`, `plan-field` (`data-item`, `data-missing`), `plan-missing` (a `state-pill` per missing item, `data-state` `missing-<item>`), `plan-cook`, `plan-cook-open`, `plan-lock-reason`, `plan-reference-open` (`data-url`); `plan-message` (`data-status`, `data-plan-id`); `plan-add` (`data-date`), `plan-add-form`, `plan-add-title`, `plan-add-time`, `plan-add-submit`.
- Gate 1: `gate-board`, `gate-change` (`data-change-key`, `data-kind`) with `gate-change-switch` and `gate-reason`, `gate-counts`, `gate-live-version`, `gate-next-version`, `gate-diff`, `gate-approve`, `gate-approved`, `gate-undo`.
- Agents: `agents-token-name`, `agents-token-create`, `agents-new-token` (`agents-new-token-value`, `agents-new-token-copy`, `agents-new-token-done`), `agents-token-row` (`data-token-name`, `data-revoked`), `agents-revoke`, `agents-revoke-confirm`; `agents-journal-row` (`data-caller`, `data-tool`, `data-result`, `data-call-id`), `agents-caller-filter`.
- Load states: `board-state-loading`, `board-state-empty`, `board-state-error` with `board-retry`; `board-refresh` reloads a ready board.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Click a row in the sidebar (Today, Funnel, My channel, Rivals, Plan, Agents), or "+" in the tab bar and a board under Lead Studio, or the Boards app menu. Gate 1 opens from Today's Gate 1 line.

## Driving it

```bash
APP_DEV=1 .claude/skills/verify/scripts/boards-lane.sh      # fake sidecar and Umami + app-up.sh --seed
.claude/skills/verify/scripts/app-drive.sh own-01 [run-id]
.claude/skills/verify/scripts/app-drive.sh rivals-01 [run-id]
.claude/skills/verify/scripts/app-drive.sh plan-01 [run-id]
.claude/skills/verify/scripts/app-drive.sh today-01 [run-id]
.claude/skills/verify/scripts/app-drive.sh agents-01 [run-id]
.claude/skills/verify/scripts/app-down.sh
```

`boards-lane.sh` starts `fake-sidecar.mjs` on a free port (it answers `/profile` with a profile built from the handle and 503 to every other path), sets `IG_SERVICE_URL` to it, starts `fake-umami.mjs` (see [connectors.md](connectors.md)), and runs `app-up.sh --seed`. Every other journey here runs on that lane too. The seed (`scripts/seed-e2e.ts`) holds the My channel rows (account `e2e_own`, reels `own-reel-1..3` and `own-carousel-1`, plan keyword `OWN01` published as `own-reel-1` with 52 DMs and 18 visits and no orders, and a draft with keyword `SALES` and 30 DMs) and the rival `@e2e_fixture_rival` with one analyzed post; rival and own post URLs are on example.com, so opening one never calls Instagram. It also holds three Plan reels for tomorrow, `plan-e2e-draft` (a bare draft), `plan-e2e-full` (script, caption, reference `e2e-rival-video-0`, 09:00) and `plan-e2e-armed` (armed with Instagram at 19:30; the publish scheduler is off on a lane, so it never posts), six month chips with long titles on other days, and this week's plan: `plan-v1` live (approved by kai) and `plan-v2` proposed by claude.

`own-01` sets the viewport to 1440x875, then prints `ok: open My channel from the new-tab menu: Overview shows the bridge's numbers, each with its source` (views, followers and DM against `getOwnBoard(30)`), `ok: Performance shows views by content type`, `ok: Content lists the window's posts from the DB with each post's own comments, shares and saves; DM is 52 by keyword, a reel with no keyword reads not connected, never 0, and a failed newest pull is marked on its row` (`sqlite3` on the lane DB; the seed gives `own-reel-3` a failed capture newer than its good one), `ok: Audience shows the follower split from the stored breakdowns`, `ok: a reel opens on Overview, and After the reel walks OWN01's funnel with checkout from Umami` (checkouts and purchases read missing: the seed has neither), `ok: back to the board, 7 days keeps only the last week's posts`, and saves `own-01-home`, `own-01-content` and `own-01-after`, each `-dark` and `-light`.

`rivals-01` prints `ok: open Rivals from the new-tab menu: the rival shows with its source, the own channel does not`, `ok: the rival's outliers are its posts at 2x or more in 30 days, best first` (against the lane DB), `ok: an evidence card opens the original post in a browser tab` (the store's browser tabs hold the post's URL), `ok: opening Rivals again comes back to the same board tab`, `ok: a handle that is not a handle is refused with its reason and writes nothing`, `ok: Add rival adds a channel after the profile check, and the new row shows` (the DB row carries the fake profile's id and follower count), `ok: a failed read shows the error state` (it renames `video_analyses` in the lane DB and clicks Refresh, then renames it back in a `finally`), `ok: Retry brings the board back`, and saves `rivals-01-dark.png`, `rivals-01-light.png` and `rivals-01-added.png`.

`plan-01` sets the viewport to 1440x875, then prints `ok: open Plan from the new-tab menu: the month shows each seeded reel on its day with its state, flagged when it misses an item`, `ok: clicking a week opens its 7 columns, each card with its post time`, `ok: a reel scheduled with Instagram cannot be dragged and says why; its day stays` (`planned_date` and `scheduled_at` read back from the lane DB), `ok: dragging a draft to another day moves it in the DB, and the week shows it there`, `ok: the month and the day follow the move`, `ok: a card opens in full: every item, not yet for key, gift, offer, and the reference reel opens in a browser tab`, `ok: an empty day takes a reel added by hand, written as a dated plan` (the DB row has the date, the time, state `scheduled` and no `scheduled_at`), and saves `plan-01-month` and `plan-01-moved`, each `-dark` and `-light`, and `plan-01-added`. It shoots `B3-06-plan-week`, `B3-07-plan-day` and `B3-08-plan-card` (the `plan-e2e-full` card) with `snapAndCompare`; the refusal must be the server's English text and the board must hold no Vietnamese letter.

`cook-01` runs on `baseline-lane.sh`, after `plan-01`. Every `app-up.sh` lane points `LEAD_STUDIO_COOK_ROOT` at `/tmp/lead-studio-cook/studio-reel`, a fresh copy of studio-reel's CLAUDE.md, AGENTS.md, workspace README and make-reel skill, never the real checkout; the journey refuses any other root. It sets Claude's command to a fake that prints its arguments and echoes input, so Claude never runs. It prints `ok: the day card of a reel with a script has Cook; Cook writes the script into studio-reel` (the file read back), `ok: Cook opens a "Reel: <title>" folder workspace on studio-reel and records it on the plan` (`cook_workspace_id` read from the lane DB), `ok: a Claude tab in the workspace starts the launch and gets /make-reel typed in` (the terminal buffer), `ok: back on the plan the card says Cooking and Open workspace returns to the reel workspace`, shoots `B5-01-cook-workspace`, and ends back on the first workspace.

`today-01` sets the viewport to 1440x875 (a 1440x900 screen less the macOS menu bar), saves funnel rows (keyword `TODAY01`) and proposes a week plan (`2099-W01`) with `mcp.sh --app`, then prints `ok: open Today from the sidebar and see every block name its source once`, `ok: every number on the board sits in a block that names its source`, `ok: checkout reads Umami as the bridge does, and with no targets the drop says so` (against `getTodayBoard`), `ok: gate counts match the lane DB` (`sqlite3` on the lane DB), `ok: overnight pulls show each connector's state from the Sources data` (against `getVaultBoard`), `ok: the board fits 1440x900 with no scroll`, and saves `today-01-dark.png` and `today-01-light.png`.

`agents-01` makes one `crowsnest_save_funnel` call with `mcp.sh --app` (reads are not journaled), then prints `ok: one MCP call with the local token shows in the journal as one ok row with caller local` (its call id appears once, its pending row hidden), `ok: the caller filter keeps only that caller's rows`, `ok: Create shows the new token once, and Done hides it for good` (the new token calls the MCP with 200), `ok: Revoke asks to confirm, then the token gets 401`, and saves `agents-01-dark.png`, `agents-01-light.png`, `agents-01-created.png`, `agents-01.png`.

## Gotchas

- `plan-01` moves the seeded draft and adds a reel, so a rerun needs a fresh lane. The drag uses Playwright `dragTo`, which drives the HTML5 drag events in Electron's Chromium.
- Opening the reference reel switches to the browser tab; `plan-01` reopens Plan from "+" to come back to the same board tab.

- The journey needs a tab bar, so the lane must have a project open, as for `shell-01`.
- The app menu is native; CDP cannot click it. The journey covers the "+" menu path only.
- A board tab is not restored after a restart.
- If `rivals-01` dies between the two renames, the lane DB keeps `video_analyses_off`; `app-down.sh` and a fresh lane reset it.
- On `boards-lane.sh` the app's background sync reaches the fake sidecar and gets 503, so the rival row shows that sync error. Without the fake sidecar, Add rival shows the sidecar-down error and writes nothing.
- Rivals' follower count, median views and outliers move only from a complete channel pull: a sync cut mid-walk leaves them as they were and marks the job `partial`. No journey drives that, because a lane makes no Instagram calls; the proof is the B1-07 test in `src/server/services/sync-engine.test.ts`. A journey run checks only that the seeded numbers still show.
- The light screenshots drop the `dark` class on `<html>` for the snap and put it back, with the app's `theme-transition-disabled` class on during the swap so the snap does not catch text mid-fade; the theme setting does not change. Each journey also waits for the "+" menu to close before its first snap.
- Token and fake-secret names carry a timestamp, so both journeys rerun on the same lane.
- `today-01` sizes the page with `setViewportSize`: Electron's CDP has no `Browser.setWindowBounds`. Each rerun proposes one more week plan version, and the gate check reads the DB, so it still holds.
- A failed newest pull on Today and Funnel shows only on the baseline lane, which seeds a failed Graph pull for today ([baseline.md](baseline.md)); `src/server/queries/funnel-board.test.ts` proves the mapping.
