Your four open questions — recommendation, not a menu
Violet or amber
Amber. Delete violet. The product exists to produce one number; the identity should be that number. Amber as --primary means every wrapped component already points at it, and it makes amber semantic: amber on a card means it beat its own baseline. Below 1.0× the number prints in ink, so the grid's amber density is the answer to "was this worth opening". Red stays for failure only — which is why amber can never mean "warning" anywhere in the app.
Fluid or wider fixed
Fluid, with a cap on prose only. Kill the 1120px wrapper entirely. Grids, tables and run history go edge to edge; the only capped element is running text — the analysis column, at 72ch. Card minimum 200px means 4 columns at 1280 and 7 at 1920 instead of 4 and 4.
Card density
Yes, but as a variant, not a second card. Card density="compact" plus data-density on the grid: min column 140px, caption dropped to one line, meta reduced to views, multiplier held at 26px. Same component, same markup, one header toggle, persisted in the URL as ?d=c so it travels with a saved view. 200 posts fit in three screens.
Type
Archivo + IBM Plex Mono. Archivo is a tight grotesque with a genuine 800 — it holds 44px without looking inflated, which the default sans does not. IBM Plex Mono takes every number that sits in a column or a row: tabular, slashed zero, so 2,272,877 and 224,403 align digit for digit and a dash reads unmistakably as a dash and not a hyphenated zero.
