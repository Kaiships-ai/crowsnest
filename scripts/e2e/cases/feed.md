## Behavior notes

- Feed page: cards show a thumbnail with caption, the outlier score as the largest number (amber from 1.0x, escalating past 5x and 20x, a dash when unscored, sorted last under the score sort), views, engagement rate, channel handle with followers, and post age. A line above the grid states the count, notes the default view, and offers "Show all". Enter or Space on a focused card opens the quick preview. List view is a plain table whose whole row is the click target.
- Filter bar: search (about 350 ms debounce), fleet, minimum score, views, engagement, timeframe, analyzed, follower band, sort, layout and density toggles, saved views dropdown, Add post URL. Layout, density, and sort do not surface Clear filters and do not ride into detail links. A single Reset clears every filter param.
- Add post URL opens the add-channel dialog on its "Paste post URL" tab. The button reads "Importing...", success closes the dialog with a "Post imported" toast and refreshes the feed.
- Quick preview: right-hand sheet titled "Quick preview" with thumbnail, score, channel, caption, counts, age, and a four-line transcript excerpt (or "No transcript yet, open the full page to transcribe."), plus Open full page and Remix. Escape, the close button, or the dimmed area close it; a reload returns to the feed with no sheet open.
- Keep this post: a star button with an optional note; kept reads "Kept"; removing carries an Undo toast. A blank note renders as a dash.
- Save to vault: Hook, Style, or Both, prefilled from the analysis and editable. No field validation, and fields do not reset on close.
- Video detail: two columns (media, score, metrics, median baseline; summary and tabs for transcript, hook and structure, why it worked, layout) with a sticky action row. Left and right arrows page to the previous and next video unless a text field or a carousel has focus. Missing numbers render as a dash, never zero. A video with no downloaded file shows a Download button; up to five similar outliers show below.
- Rendering needs no external service. Only the download action launched from the detail page reaches the sidecar, and Add post URL reaches yt-dlp.
- Edge cases: two tabs racing a keep or a saved view change are refused, not duplicated; re-importing a post updates it in place.

## feed: test cases

Result markers: 🟢 pass, 🔴 bug, 🟡 blocked (missing dependency named in the cell). Rows still reading "not run" were not driven in QA round 1.

How to run this file: start the dev server and open `http://localhost:3001` (never `127.0.0.1`), on a database with at least one tracked channel that has finished a sync, so the feed has scored videos to show. Seed a mix: at least one analyzed video and one unanalyzed one, one outlier scoring at or above 1.0 and one below, one video with a transcript and one without, one carousel post, and one video you can soft remove (set `deletedAt`) to check the removed paths. Analyzed videos are the precondition for the detail page actions, so an analyzed video is needed to reach most of the quick preview, keep, save-to-vault, and video detail rows. To reach a clean feed state, open a bare `/feed` (the High Conviction view applies) or click Show all for the whole library; note the results count you start from so a Load more or a filter change reads as a real change. The feed, the filter bar, the quick preview, and rendering the detail page need no external service. The rows that do need a service say so in the Needs column: the add post import needs the post-fetch provider reachable. Offline route and action tests run with `vitest` against a temporary SQLite database, covering `saved-view-actions`, `saved-post-actions`, and `queries/feed.ts`. Browse-e2e cases are driven with the `browse` skill against the running dev server, per `references/browse-e2e-qa.md`; none of the `data-testid` values the journeys below need exist in the current markup yet, so those cases are blocked on adding them first. "A soft removed video" means a video row with `deletedAt` set. "A second tab" shares the session; it is not a second browser.

## 4. security-lite

The feed reaches no external service of its own and gates nothing behind a license or a role; its only outbound call is the "Add post URL" import's post-fetch provider request, which sends only the pasted URL, no buyer or channel data of its own. The feed has no secret to handle and no sidecar token to check.
