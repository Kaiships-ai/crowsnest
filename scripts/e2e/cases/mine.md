### Behavior notes

- `checkGraphTokenAction` refreshes the cached token state.
- A missing metric is null, never zero. Rates divide by reach and are null when reach is missing or zero.
- Publish: mp4 only, caption capped at 2200 characters, refused until a video, a caption, the Graph token, and the storage bucket are all present. Retry is refused past the attempt cap.
- Measurement: `measureOwnChannelAction` and the hourly scheduler share one lock and budget (190 and 150 calls, less recent calls). A rate limit stops the run and keeps rows written. Reels are due hourly for 72 hours, then daily, with failure backoff.
