# Crowsnest design pass (visual)

Report-only browser pass against `main` / `8909788`, dev server at http://localhost:3000.
Nothing was fixed, no code changed, no commits, no branch switches. HEAD did not move
during the pass.

Method: gstack browse (headed Chromium) plus computed-style queries against the live DOM.
Screenshots under `.gstack/browse-cwd/design/`. Every measurement below is a number read
off the rendered page, not an estimate.

Lens: visual and aesthetic. Functional bugs already filed in
`docs/reviews/2026-08-04-qa.md` are not repeated; where a design finding sits next to a QA
one, it is cross-referenced.

Screens covered: dashboard, feed (grid and list), video detail (Transcript and Hook &
structure tabs, Layout section populated and absent), runs, vault, settings (11 sections),
plus dark mode and three viewport widths.

## The design system, as rendered

Worth stating first, because it is good and most of the findings below are drift away from
it rather than the absence of a system.

- Two typefaces: Archivo for UI, IBM Plex Mono for numerals, labels and technical values.
  No third font, no system-ui fallback in use.
- Warm neutral palette. Page `rgb(244, 242, 239)`, ink `rgb(23, 21, 15)`, surfaces
  `rgb(234, 231, 225)` and `rgb(226, 223, 216)`. Warm at every step in light mode.
- One accent, amber `rgb(168, 95, 0)`, plus a single semantic green `rgb(47, 107, 74)`.
- The accent carries meaning. A score at or above 1.0x renders amber, below 1.0x renders
  gray, and the same rule holds on the feed cards, the ranking list, the scatter and the
  similar-outliers row. That is a real encoding, applied consistently.
- Thumbnails are deliberately desaturated (`grayscale(1) contrast(1.06)`), with hover
  raising contrast rather than restoring color. The video player itself plays in color.
- No AI-slop patterns. No purple gradient, no three-column icon-in-circle feature grid, no
  decorative blobs, no uniform bubbly radius, no centered-everything. This app does not
  look generated.
- The engineering underneath is sound: 3 `focus-visible` rules, zero orphaned
  `outline: none`, zero `transition: all`, 3 `prefers-reduced-motion` blocks. Console is
  clean.

Settings is the best-built screen in the app: one `h1`, eleven `h2`, a sticky anchor nav,
honest risk copy, mono for every technical value.

## Findings

### High

#### H1. Frame pass has no user interface at all on 444 of the 452 tracked videos

Screen: `/feed/<videoId>`, "Hook & structure" tab.

Steps:
1. Open `/feed/7c810d0b-7fc6-43e1-a0a6-da2cd0d4fff2` (any video that has never been
   analyzed).
2. Click the "Hook & structure" tab.
3. Search the page for the feature.

Expected: the Layout section in its never-run state, with the "Run frame pass" button.

Actual: neither the string `LAYOUT` nor the string `frame pass` appears anywhere in the
rendered page. Verified by regex over `document.body.innerText`; both return false. The
tab contains one amber "Transcribe & Analyze" button sitting in 92px of empty space below
the tab row, and nothing else. The Layout section only mounts once a `video_analyses` row
exists.

Only 8 of 452 videos have a downloaded asset and all 8 already carry a successful pass, so
in practice the feature this session shipped is invisible on 98% of the catalog. QA M4
found the button is one tab away from anything that mentions it; the stronger version is
that on almost every video there is no button to find.

Fix: mount the Layout section whenever the video row exists, in its never-run state, rather
than gating it on the analysis row. The never-run state is where the button, the
"needs a downloaded video" precondition and the value proposition all belong.

#### H2. The producer donut recycles four colors across nine producers, so three of them share one swatch

Screen: `/`, "Who produces them".

Legend swatch colors read off the DOM:

| producer | swatch |
| --- | --- |
| @personalbrandlaunch | `rgb(23, 21, 15)` |
| @nateherkai | `rgb(176, 171, 161)` |
| @jun_yuh | `rgb(87, 85, 79)` |
| @danmartell | `rgb(127, 124, 117)` |
| @kallaway | `rgb(23, 21, 15)` |
| @mavgpt | `rgb(176, 171, 161)` |
| @nick_saraev | `rgb(87, 85, 79)` |
| @hormozi | `rgb(127, 124, 117)` |
| @chase.h.ai | `rgb(23, 21, 15)` |

Three producers are painted the identical near-black. Two more pairs share a value. No
segment on the ring can be mapped back to a name, which is the only thing a donut is for.
Two of the four base grays, `rgb(87, 85, 79)` and `rgb(127, 124, 117)`, are barely
separable from each other anyway at the 8px swatch size.

The counts are 8, 7, 7, 7, 6, 6, 6, 6, 6. Nine near-equal slices is the worst possible case
for a ring.

Fix: drop the donut for a ranked horizontal bar list. The legend already is that list; give
each row a bar and delete the ring. If the ring stays, it needs direct labels on the
segments and nine distinguishable steps.

#### H3. The ranking list bars are clamped so hard they encode almost nothing

Screen: `/`, "Ranking, 113 scored".

Measured bar widths against their values:

| score | bar width |
| --- | --- |
| 116.5x | 96.0px |
| 15.2x | 96.0px |
| 14.5x | 96.0px |
| 13.9x | 96.0px |
| 13.3x | 96.0px |
| 7.7x | 85.6px |
| 5.8x | 78.9px |
| 3.7x | 68.4px |

The top five are pinned at the maximum and are pixel-identical. A 31-fold spread (3.7x to
116.5x) compresses into a 1.4-fold visual spread. The bar next to 116.5x and the bar next
to 13.3x are the same object.

This is the same clamp family as QA L3 (the 12x median axis on the detail page). Here the
consequence is worse, because a bar chart that does not vary reads as decoration attached
to a number rather than as a broken scale, so nobody suspects it.

Fix: log scale the bar, or delete it. The mono numerals are already right-aligned and
carry the ranking on their own.

#### H4. The post caption is set at display size in a 320px column, producing a 2,343px wall of text

Screen: `/feed/<videoId>`, left column below the median chart.

Measured: `font-size: 24px`, `font-weight: 600`, `line-height: 33px`, column width 320px,
2,052 characters, rendered height 2,343px. That is roughly 27 characters per line against
a 45 to 75 target, and 2.4 full screen heights of semibold 24px type.

The same text appears in the Transcript tab at 16px regular in a 492px column, where it
reads fine. The caption is reference material and it is typeset like a headline.

Fix: 15px or 16px regular, and clamp to about six lines with a "show more" control. The
measure problem is fixed by the size change alone.

#### H5. The Layout section leads with a raw dump that buries the three fields worth reading

Screen: `/feed/<videoId>`, "Hook & structure" tab, Layout section, populated state.

Measured heights of the four value rows:

| field | rendered height | characters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ON-SCREEN TEXT | 364px | 964 |
| TEXT POSITION | 57px | 175 |
| CUT RHYTHM | 28px | 108 |
| FRAMING | 114px | 292 |

ON-SCREEN TEXT is a comma-run of every caption fragment the model saw: `'and'`, `'The'`,
`'is now'`, `'Can you help me'`, and about forty more, in straight single quotes that
clash with the curly quotes used everywhere else in the app. It is 1.8 times taller than
the other three fields combined, it is the first thing in the section, and it pushes
TEXT POSITION, CUT RHYTHM and FRAMING (the actual reading) below the fold.

The section's hierarchy is inverted: the least summarized field gets the most space.

Fix: clamp ON-SCREEN TEXT to two or three lines with an expander, or render the fragments
as small chips in a scrollable strip. Either way the summarized fields should come first.

### Medium

#### M1. The feed toolbar renders two separate toggle groups as one four-icon run, and two of the four look selected

Screen: `/feed`, top right.

Four 36x28 icon buttons at x = 1518, 1562, 1606, 1650, with an identical 8px gap between
every pair. They are actually two independent two-state groups:

| button | aria-label | aria-pressed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Grid view | true |
| 2 | List view | false |
| 3 | Comfortable density | true |
| 4 | Compact density | false |

Because the gap across the group boundary equals the gap inside each group, the run reads
as a single four-way radio, and a single-select control showing two filled buttons reads
as broken. None of the four has a `title`, so a mouse user cannot learn what any icon
means without clicking it.

Fix: widen the gap between the two groups (or add a divider) so the grouping is visible,
and add `title` attributes.

#### M2. Four of eight routes ship two `h1` elements, and two of them show the brand name where the page name belongs

Heading sweep across the shell:

| route | h1 elements |
| --- | --- |
| `/` | Dashboard @24px |
| `/feed` | Outlier Feed @24px |
| `/channels` | Channels @24px |
| `/settings` | Settings @24px, then 11 `h2` |
| `/mine` | My channel @24px, @kaiships.ai @24px |
| `/vault` | Vault @24px, Vault @34px |
| `/runs` | Crowsnest @24px, Runs @24px |
| `/logs` | Crowsnest @24px, Logs @24px |

The surface map states the topbar owns the `h1` and pages start at `h2`. Dashboard, feed,
channels and settings follow it. The other four do not.

Two distinct problems:

1. On `/runs` and `/logs` the topbar falls back to "Crowsnest" because `ROUTE_TITLES` has
   no entry for them. Every other screen answers "what page am I on" in the topbar; these
   two answer it with the app name.
2. `/vault` renders the word "Vault" twice, 72px apart, and the page copy at 34px is
   larger than the topbar at 24px, so the element the system says is subordinate is the
   visually dominant one.

Fix: add `/runs` and `/logs` to `ROUTE_TITLES`, and demote the in-page duplicates to `h2`
or drop them where the topbar already says it.

#### M3. The most-used secondary text token fails WCAG AA at its most common size

`rgb(127, 124, 117)` at 13px measures 3.73:1 on the page background and 3.37:1 on the
sidebar. AA needs 4.5:1 for text this size. It appears about 44 times per screen and
carries the sidebar group labels (RESEARCH, CREATE, SOURCES, SYSTEM), every mono eyebrow
(LAYOUT, ON-SCREEN TEXT, TODAY'S TOP OUTLIERS), card metadata, and the keyboard hint.

The amber `rgb(168, 95, 0)` at 13px measures 4.37:1, which also just misses.

This is one token, not 44 fixes. Darkening it to roughly `rgb(103, 100, 94)` clears AA and
barely changes the look.

#### M4. The beat duration bars are styled as dividers, so nobody will read them as data

Screen: `/feed/<videoId>`, "Hook & structure" tab, BEATS list.

Each beat carries a 2px rule under it. Measured widths against beat durations:

| beat | duration | bar |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 to 0:10 | 10s | 51.2px |
| 0:10 to 0:30 | 20s | 102.3px |
| 0:30 to 0:36 | 6s | 30.7px |
| 0:36 to 1:04 | 28s | 143.3px |
| 1:04 to 1:32 | 28s | 143.3px |
| 1:32 to 1:51 | 19s | 97.2px |

Perfectly proportional at 5.12 px per second. The encoding is correct. The problem is that
it is rendered with `class="mt-3 h-0.5 bg-line-strong"`, which is the divider token, in the
divider position (bottom of a list item, after a margin), at the divider color. Every
visual cue says separator. Good data wearing the wrong clothes.

Fix: give the bars a faint full-width track behind them so they read as filled against a
total, move them above the beat text or next to the timestamp, and use a color that is not
the rule color.

#### M5. The detail page player drops in unstyled native video controls

Screen: `/feed/<videoId>`.

The player uses Chrome's default control bar: its own dark chrome, its own type, its own
progress styling and a "more options" menu the app does not control. On a page this
carefully typeset it is the single most jarring element, and the native menu offers
Download and Picture in Picture, which are not app affordances.

Fix: a minimal custom control bar (play, scrub, time, mute, fullscreen) in the app's own
tokens, or at least `::-webkit-media-controls` styling to match the palette.

#### M6. Two different tab components for the same job

`/vault` tabs: mono, uppercase, 13px, active marked by an underline, no track.
`/feed/<videoId>` tabs: Archivo, sentence case, active marked by a white pill inside a
beige track.

Same interaction, same page role, two unrelated designs. Pick one and delete the other.
The pill version reads better against the app's card surfaces.

#### M7. The settings anchor nav marks two sections active at once

Screen: `/settings`, scrolled to any section past the first.

Scrolled to "Runs and alerts", two links carry both the active background
`rgb(226, 223, 216)` and `aria-current="true"`: "Niche profile" and "Runs and alerts". The
other nine are clean. The scrollspy never clears the initial item, so on a screen whose
whole purpose is telling you where you are in an eleven-section scroll, it points at two
places.

#### M8. The runs table carries a column that is empty in every row, and encodes status only as a 6px colored dot

Screen: `/runs`.

Headers are `Run, Type, Started, Duration, Progress, (unnamed), Actions`. The Progress
column is populated in 0 of 50 rows. One header is an empty string. So of seven columns,
one is dead and one is unlabeled.

Status is a 6px dot, red or green, to the left of the run title. There is no text status,
no icon shape difference, and nothing else distinguishes a failed run from a successful
one except reading the gray subtitle. That is color-only encoding on the one signal the
screen exists to convey.

Separately, failed rows show "Retry" where successful rows show "View", so a failed run
offers no path to its own trace. Combined with QA H2 (run trace is nearly empty), a user
who sees a failure has neither a link nor a log.

Fix: drop or populate Progress, name the unnamed column, and pair the dot with a text
status. Give failed rows both View and Retry.

#### M9. Dark mode is a lightness inversion: the surface hierarchy and the palette warmth do not survive it

Measured in dark mode: page `rgb(11, 11, 12)`, elevated card `rgb(20, 20, 22)`. A nine-value
step out of 255, with a 1px border doing most of the separation work. In light mode the
same pair is `rgb(244, 242, 239)` against `rgb(226, 223, 216)`, an 18-value step plus a
border, and the tiles read as objects on a page. In dark they read as outlines drawn on
the background.

The palette also flips temperature. Light mode is warm at every step (R greater than G
greater than B). Dark mode page is `rgb(11, 11, 12)`, where blue exceeds red. The brand
warmth is a light-mode-only property.

Body text is `rgb(242, 242, 240)`, close to pure white on near-black.

One more: on the detail page in dark mode the outlier score renders in two different
ambers, `rgb(192, 132, 47)` for the thumbnail pill at 19px and `rgb(232, 163, 61)` for the
headline at 52px. Light mode uses one amber plus white-on-amber for the pill.

Fix: raise the elevated surface to about `rgb(26, 26, 28)` and keep the warm bias
(`rgb(18, 17, 16)` page, `rgb(28, 27, 25)` card), soften body text toward
`rgb(224, 222, 218)`, and make the pill use one amber token.

#### M10. The feed loading skeleton is not the shape of the card it becomes

Screen: `/feed`, first paint.

The skeleton card is image block, then a circular avatar next to one short line, then two
lines. The loaded card is image block with a caption overlay, then a large amber score,
then a metadata line, then a bordered action row with a Remix button. The avatar circle
does not exist in the real card; the action row does not exist in the skeleton. There is
no shimmer or pulse animation anywhere (`animate`, `pulse` and `shimmer` classes all
return zero matches), so it is static gray blocks.

The filter bar skeleton is three stacked bars of decreasing width, a generic paragraph
shape, where the real content is a search field plus five dropdowns plus a chip band.

Fix: match the block shapes to what lands, and add a pulse.

#### M11. The caption overlay on feed thumbnails covers the reel's own on-screen hook text

Screen: `/feed`, grid view.

The app paints a hard-edged black caption box over the upper third of every thumbnail.
That is exactly where reels put their own hook text. On the visible row, the app's overlay
sits directly under or across the video's own "ChatGPT applied to" and "ChatGPT can find
your" burned-in text, so two competing text layers occupy the same zone and the frame the
user is trying to judge is the one hidden.

For a tool whose purpose is reading what made a video work, occluding the on-screen text
with a duplicate of the caption is the wrong trade.

Fix: move the caption below the image, or reveal it on hover only, or shift the overlay to
the lower third where reels rarely place hook text.

#### M12. The median baseline bar fills its whole axis, so it never shows where this post sits

Screen: `/feed/<videoId>`, under the big score.

The bar is a solid amber fill spanning the full width, with axis labels 0.2x, 1.0x =
215.1K, and 12x, and a single tick at the 1.0x position. Nothing marks the post's own
value. A reader sees "the entire range is highlighted", not "this post is here". QA L3
covers the 12x clamp against a 116.5x value; this is the separate problem that even an
in-range value would not be locatable.

Fix: draw the track in a neutral and fill only to the post's value, with a distinct marker
at 1.0x.

#### M13. Settings puts its largest gap in the meaningless place

Screen: `/settings`, 1710px wide.

Measured: app sidebar ends at x=240. The anchor nav starts at x=439 and ends at x=615. The
content starts at x=655.

So there is a 199px empty band between the two navigation columns, and only a 40px gap
between the anchor nav and the content it controls. The widest whitespace on the page
separates nothing; the tightest separates the two things that need separating. The result
reads as two disconnected nav columns with a void between them.

Fix: pull the anchor nav left against the sidebar (or into it), and widen the
nav-to-content gutter to at least 64px.

#### M14. The hook chips use two containers for one kind of value

Screen: `/feed/<videoId>`, "Hook & structure" tab, HOOK row.

| value | background | padding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| relevance-hook | transparent | 0px |
| product-announcement | `rgb(226, 223, 216)` | 2px 8px |
| explainer | `rgb(226, 223, 216)` | 2px 8px |
| demo-walkthrough | `rgb(226, 223, 216)` | 2px 8px |

Same font, same size, same text color, different container. The unchipped first item reads
as a label introducing the chips after it, when it is actually a peer value. A second row
below it uses a third treatment again (plain mono with a trailing `unverified`), so one row
of tags carries three visual grammars and the only explanation is the word "unverified".

Fix: one chip treatment for values, with the unverified ones differentiated by a border or
reduced opacity rather than by losing their container.

#### M15. Nine font sizes, four of them crowded into a five-pixel band

Counted on the video detail page:

| size | elements |
| --- | --- |
| 11px | 1 |
| 12.8px | 1 |
| 13px | 68 |
| 15px | 12 |
| 16px | 30 |
| 17px | 34 |
| 19px | 3 |
| 24px | 8 |
| 52px | 1 |

16px and 17px are both used heavily and are one pixel apart, so they create no perceptible
hierarchy while guaranteeing that similar content looks subtly misaligned. 12.8px appears
once (the detail page action buttons), which makes the buttons the smallest type on the
screen apart from a single 11px element. Step ratios run 1.15, 1.07, 1.06, 1.12, 1.26,
2.17: not a scale.

Fix: collapse 15/16/17 to a single body size, drop 12.8px in favour of 13px, and pick a
ratio for the rest.

#### M16. There is no responsive layout below roughly 1024px

At 1280x720 the app is fine. At 768x1024 the sidebar stays at its full 240px, the detail
page keeps its two columns, and the right column squeezes to about 120px so the transcript
renders one or two words per line, the tab row clips mid-word ("Transcript | Ho"), and the
Remix button is cut off at the viewport edge. At 375x812 the sidebar still does not
collapse, the topbar title wraps behind it, and content clips mid-word: "444.4K follow",
"0.0% engageme", "116.".

For a local desktop tool this may be a deliberate scope call, and QA listed mobile as
untested rather than broken. Worth making it a stated decision: either add a breakpoint
that collapses the sidebar, or set a minimum width and say so.

#### M17. A developer note ships in red in the settings UI, above a control that still works

Screen: `/settings`, "Runs and alerts".

Under "Remix SLA budget": "Not wired yet. Stored and validated, but no run raises a
notification off this number until sync-engine.ts is wired to it." Rendered in the app's
error red, which everywhere else means something went wrong rather than something is not
built. It also names a source file to a user who has no reason to know it.

The number input beside it is fully enabled, so the UI invites you to set a value it has
just told you does nothing.

Fix: neutral "not active yet" styling, drop the file name, and disable the input until it
is wired.

#### M18. The transcript panel clips text mid-glyph with no fade

Screen: `/feed/<videoId>`, Transcript tab. Reproduces at 1280 and 1710 wide.

The transcript is a fixed-height scroll region whose bottom edge cuts horizontally through
the letterforms of the last visible line. There is no gradient, fade or shadow marking the
boundary, so it reads as a rendering fault rather than as more content below. The scrollbar
is the only hint.

Fix: a short fade mask at the bottom edge, or size the container to whole line-heights.

#### M19. Two different treatments for "something is wrong"

The settings risk callout uses a red shield icon with a heading and body copy on a neutral
panel. The dashboard dependency panel uses a red left border with no icon, and encodes the
per-item status as small dots that differ only in gray value (a light gray dot for
"Instagram sidecar is up", a dark gray dot for "Model backend answered, but generation
isn't confirmed"). No hue, no shape, no icon distinguishes a healthy dependency from a
degraded one.

Fix: one warning pattern with an icon, and give the dependency dots the semantic green and
amber the palette already defines.

#### M20. The undownloaded-video placeholder makes both layers unreadable

Screen: `/feed/<videoId>` for a video with no downloaded asset.

A semi-transparent white panel carrying "Video not downloaded yet" and a "Download video"
button sits over the thumbnail. The thumbnail's own burned-in text ("Claude just killed")
shows straight through the panel behind the words, so neither string is comfortably
legible. A play triangle sits above the message and reads as clickable.

Fix: a solid or heavily blurred backdrop behind the message, and drop the play triangle
from a state where playing is not possible.

### Low

#### L1. `instagrapidrives`

`/settings`, Instagram connection, risk copy. A mono `instagrapi` span runs straight into
the next word with no space. Confirmed in `innerText` as a single token.

#### L2. The command palette placeholder uses three dots where the topbar uses an ellipsis

The topbar search button uses the single ellipsis character. The command palette
placeholder reads "Search for a command to run" followed by three periods. Same control
family, two conventions.

#### L3. Mixed apostrophes inside one panel

The dashboard dependency panel has "generation isn't confirmed" with a straight apostrophe
in the heading and "doesn't call" with a curly one in the body directly beneath it. The
page as a whole runs 27 curly to 11 straight, and the straight ones outside user-generated
captions are all app copy.

#### L4. A long dash inside the scatter caption

The dashboard scatter caption joins "missing a score or a view count" to "reach on a log
scale" with an unspaced em dash, which then breaks across a line. The repo's own writing
rules forbid that character in prose, and UI copy is prose.

#### L5. Mixed spelling conventions

`/vault` sort control reads "Analysed first" (British). `/feed/<videoId>` reads "Transcribe
& Analyze" (American). Pick one.

#### L6. The vault sort control renders above an empty list

`/vault` with zero saved posts still shows "SORT | Recently saved | Highest score |
Analysed first" between the tabs and the empty state, offering four ways to order nothing.

#### L7. The card overflow menu is too narrow for its own items

`/feed`, card "More actions" menu. The panel is sized so that "View on Instagram" wraps to
two lines while "Copy link" fits on one. A menu should size to its widest item.

#### L8. The dashboard section labels use two different tokens

"Dependencies" renders as a `<p>` with `text-muted-foreground tracking-wide`, letter
spacing 0.325px. Every other section label on the same screen ("Today's top outliers",
"Reach vs score", "Coverage", "Recently viewed", "Fleets with new spikes") renders as a
`<span>` with `text-faint`, letter spacing 0.78px. Same visual role, two token sets, one
screen.

None of them is a heading element, which is why the dashboard has zero `h2` (see M2).

#### L9. Recently-viewed card metadata wraps badly and leaves the row unaligned

`/`, "Recently viewed". The metadata wraps so that separator dots strand at line ends
("@kallaway ." / "444.4K followers ." / "26d ago") and one card starts a line with a
leading separator. Three cards run to four lines and one to three, so the card bottoms in a
single row do not align.

Fix: keep each separator non-breaking with its preceding token, and stretch the cards to a
common height.

#### L10. The follower band hides its empty bucket, so the scale looks complete when it is not

`/feed`: "Nano <10K 9", "Mid 100K-1M 97", "Macro 1M+ 346". The counts sum to 452, so the
10K to 100K range is empty and is simply not rendered. Presented as three ordered buckets
with no gap shown, the band reads as the whole scale.

Fix: render the empty bucket disabled with a 0, so the ordering stays honest.

#### L11. Buttons are the smallest type on the video detail page

"Keep this post", "Save to Vault" and "Copy as prompt" render at 12.8px, smaller than the
13px mono labels and the 15/16/17px body. Interactive elements should not be the smallest
thing on a screen.

#### L12. Caption emoji render in full color in an app that forcibly desaturates every thumbnail

`/feed`, both densities. Thumbnails carry `grayscale(1)` but caption emoji (the pointing
hand, the laughing face) render at full saturation, making them the most colorful thing on
a monochrome page and pulling the eye to the least important content.

#### L13. Two search fields with different affordances, 60px apart

`/feed` has a bare underlined "Search captions and channels" input directly below the
topbar's bordered pill search button. Same page, same word, two visual grammars for what a
search field looks like.

#### L14. The runs live indicator has no semantic color

`/runs` shows a pulsing 6px dot next to "Live", colored `rgb(23, 21, 15)`, the same
near-black as body text. The animation is the only signal, so a still frame (or a
reduced-motion user) cannot tell a live SSE connection from a dead one. The run status dots
on the same screen do use hue, so liveness and status speak different languages.

#### L15. The settings anchor labels do not match their section headings

Nav "Instagram" leads to "Instagram connection". Nav "Graph API" leads to "Instagram Graph
API". Nav "API keys" leads to "API keys & secrets". Eight of eleven match exactly; three
are renamed, which costs a beat of re-reading on arrival.

#### L16. Nine of the runs rows are titled identically

`/runs` shows "Channel sync" nine times in the scannable title column, with the
distinguishing detail (channel count, new, updated) demoted to the gray subtitle and the
channel name absent entirely. The most prominent text is the least informative.

## Quick wins
