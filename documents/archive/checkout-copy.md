# Crowsnest

Everyone says study what is working. Nobody has time to scroll forty accounts and guess. Crowsnest finds the posts already blowing up in your niche and turns one into your next script, so you copy what is proven instead of hoping. It runs on your Mac, on your own data, with no subscription.

**$99 once. Lifetime license, two activations.**

## Find the posts that are actually working

Every post in your feed is scored against that account's own baseline, not a generic industry number. A creator who normally pulls 5,000 views and suddenly hits 200,000 shows up as a clear outlier, while a big account's routine hit does not drown out a small account's breakout. The feed sorts by that score first, so you open the tool and see what is actually worth your time instead of scrolling for it.

Point Crowsnest at the Instagram creators in your niche and it syncs their recent posts straight to your Mac: no scraper subscription, no data leaving your machine.

## See why it worked, then keep it

Open any outlier and ask Crowsnest to analyze it: the hook type, the structure beat by beat, and a plain explanation of why it worked.

When a hook or a structure is worth keeping, build your own swipe file of proven patterns. Save it to your Vault and pull it into any future remix, so a good pattern is not a one time read, it is something you build a library out of.

## Turn it into your script

Turn a winning post into a full script in your own voice. Set what your version is about, review the research pulled from the source, pick or generate a hook, choose a structure, then generate the script. You steer every step, and nothing moves forward without your say.

No API key yet? Get a ready to paste prompt built from everything you set up, hook, structure, and all, for ChatGPT, Claude.ai, or any model you already use, and write the script there, free.

## What you need

- A Mac.
- Your own Instagram login, so Crowsnest can sync the channels you track.
- An Anthropic API key, only if you want Crowsnest to run the analysis and the AI generation steps for you. Tracking, scoring, research, and the ready to paste prompt all work without one.
