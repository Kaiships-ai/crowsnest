# crowsnest product document

Crowsnest is a $99, one-time Mac Kit for a solo Instagram creator who wants an edge from
watching what already works elsewhere, without scanning a dozen accounts by hand. It tracks
Instagram creators, surfaces viral outliers scored against each channel's own baseline,
analyzes them with a language model, and runs a remix pipeline that turns an outlier into a
script in the buyer's own voice. Buyers get a Polar license key and install with one command;
everything after that runs against the buyer's own local database, on their own Mac. There is
no server crowsnest owns, no subscription, and no second seat.

The core loop is one paragraph: an outlier appears in the feed because the IG sidecar synced a
tracked channel; the buyer analyzes it to see its hook, structure, and why it worked; a remix
takes that analysis through six stages, research, hook, style, script, and finish, to a script
on a dated plan; the studio cooks that plan into a rendered video; the buyer publishes it, or
marks it handled, on their own terms.

The persona this serves is the same one crowsnest's own founder built it for: someone who opens
the app, sees which watched posts beat their own channel overnight, reads why one worked, and
turns it into their next post, rather than scrolling and guessing. (This document checked
`character-spec.md` at HEAD as the named persona source; that file is an unfilled visual
character-design template with no persona content, so the intent above is drawn instead from
the product description's own framing of its single user. See
`brd/crowsnest/01-concept-research.md` section 3 for the full note.)

Full detail: `brd/crowsnest/01-concept-research.md` (the problem, the persona, the actor model,
