The rule

  1. Keep the sprite exact. Do not move, resize, recolor, or drop a single rect.
  2. Express an action only through transforms: x, y, rotation, scale, svgOrigin, plus display toggles.
  3. Closed or blinking eyes are a scaleY squash on the real eye rects.
  4. Accessories are added as separate elements around the sprite. A laptop, a lightbulb, and floating Z's are fine. Editing the body to fake them is not.
  5. Build every scene from claude-sprite.js so there is one source and no drift.
