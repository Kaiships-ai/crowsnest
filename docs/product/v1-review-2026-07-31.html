The worst one, now fixed

Opening /feed silently applied a saved preset with a minimum score of 5. The control next to it read "Any score". Seven of your eight posts were hidden with nothing on screen explaining why, and the count said "1 result".

That is not a missing feature. It is the screen the product exists for telling you the opposite of what it did. It survived a full day of review because it typechecks perfectly. Fixed in 5b5a846: the row now reads "5x+".

Why you cannot use it tomorrow

Getting from "open Crowsnest" to "have a script" needs two background processes you start by hand, in a terminal, every session: the Instagram sidecar, and a working LLM backend. Neither auto-starts. One carries real account-ban risk.

Everything downstream of those two works. The one job is built. It just does not start itself.

LAYER 4Backend, and the brain you said was unclear

You were right that the brain is unclear. It fails at two points, and neither of them is Crowsnest.

The brain, diagnosed

Point one. 9router was not running. Its launch agent was loaded but exiting with status 78, a config error. I started it by hand and the proxy is up on :20128 now.

Point two. With it up, the brain still cannot answer. CLAUDE_MODEL is nvidia/minimaxai/minimax-m2.7, which returns 410 Gone, end of life. So does nvidia/z-ai/glm4.7. The 36 cx/* models return 401, token expired, please sign in again.

Crowsnest is wired correctly: the key resolves, ping succeeds, the request reaches the router. Your router's upstream auth expired and the model you pointed at was retired. There is no fallback, because your ANTHROPIC_API_KEY is a 35-character router token, not an sk-ant- key.

The thing most likely to bite you first

The sync engine is the most heavily tested code in the repo and the least proven against reality. Rate limits, stale locks, cancel mid-request, retry budgets: all covered against a mock, none exercised against the real sidecar for more than 32 seconds. Your next action is almost certainly a real multi-channel sync.

What the score means

This is not a bad product. It is a nearly finished one with two unplugged cables and a habit of trusting its own static checks. The measurement half is genuinely good and the numbers survive cross-checking against the database. The creation half is complete in code and unreachable in practice.

A 6.2 that becomes an 8 the moment two processes start is a different situation from a 6.2 that needs another month of building.

NEXTWhat to do, in order

Yours, and nothing else can do them

  • Re-authenticate 9router. Right-click its tray icon, open the dashboard, sign in again. Then set CLAUDE_MODEL to a live cx/* model. Every nvidia/* model on your router is retired. This single step unblocks remix, the run log, and the frame pass question.
  • Get a Graph API token with insights permissions. That is the whole of /mine.
  • Run one real multi-channel sync and watch it. It is the least proven path in the app and the first thing you will actually do.

Mine, and worth doing before you rely on it

  • Make the app start its own dependencies, or say plainly on screen that they are down with a one-click path. Two terminal commands per session is the single biggest thing between this and daily use.
  • Restore the suggested-channel picker in onboarding, so picking a niche in step 1 feeds step 3 instead of a blank text box.
  • Pull own-metrics.ts's hardcoded minimum and MAX_ITEMS_PER_SYNC into the settings module, so there is one source of truth per idea.
  • Add a backup step to db:migrate. Three forward-only migrations went onto the live database today with no snapshot.

Decide, do not build yet

  • Layout reads. A third of the stated mission, and the answer changes the product rather than the schedule. If your router cannot carry images, this needs a different model and that is a cost decision. Nothing should be built on top of it until the spike runs.
