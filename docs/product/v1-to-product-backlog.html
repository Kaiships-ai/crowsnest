The honest read

Six items stop you using this. Three of them are you signing into things. One is a decision. Two are code, and both are about the app starting itself rather than about the app doing its job.

Groups C, D and E are 19 items that make it better, not usable. If you never did any of them, the product would still do the one job, assuming A is done.

The one I would not defer

E1, the backup step on db:migrate. It is the smallest item on this page and the only one whose absence is unrecoverable. Everything else costs time when it goes wrong. That one costs your data.
